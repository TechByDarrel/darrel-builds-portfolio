About Me

Frontend Developer with a Passion for Creating Digital Experiences.

I'm Darrel, a frontend developer from Nigeria who enjoys transforming ideas into modern, responsive, and engaging web experiences. I believe great websites should not only look beautiful but also provide excellent usability and performance.

Over the past few months, I have designed and developed multiple responsive websites across different industries, including restaurant, healthcare, e-commerce, real estate, and AI SaaS. Every project has helped me improve my design thinking, frontend architecture, and attention to detail.

I enjoy learning new technologies, solving challenging problems, and continuously improving my skills. My goal is to become a world-class full-stack software engineer while building products that make a real impact.

I'm also building a strong foundation in cybersecurity. I recently completed Google's Foundations of Cybersecurity course and Cisco's Introduction to Cybersecurity, and I built PhishGuard, a tool that helps identify suspicious and phishing URLs. I care about writing code that is tested and security-minded.
